# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Administrators managing authentication configurations, user accounts, OAuth clients, and security settings.

## Product Purpose
An authentication provider suite and administrative control panel for managing access, identities, and security policies.

## Brand Personality
Secure, calm, authoritative, trustworthy, clear.

## Anti-references
Flashy marketing SaaS pages, neon dashboards, overly complex technical panes.

## Design Principles
- Security through clarity
- Expert confidence
- Show, don't tell

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance. High contrast form inputs, clear focus states for keyboard navigation, clear error states.
